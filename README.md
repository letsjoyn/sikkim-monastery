# SoulOfSikkim: Digitizing Sikkim's Sacred Heritage

## Project Overview

SoulOfSikkim is a digital platform dedicated to preserving and sharing the spiritual and cultural heritage of Sikkim's monasteries. By combining immersive technology with deep respect for Buddhist traditions, the project aims to make sacred sites accessible to a global audience, support local communities, and promote sustainable tourism.

## Key Features

- **360° Virtual Tours:** High-resolution, interactive tours of monastery interiors with multi-language audio narration (English, Hindi, Nepali, Tibetan, Japanese, French).
- **Interactive Map:** Geo-tagged monastery locations, travel routes, real-time weather, and nearby attractions.
- **Digital Archives:** Scanned manuscripts, murals, and documents with AI-powered search and categorization for researchers and the public.
- **Cultural Calendar:** Up-to-date festival and event listings with booking and participation options.
- **Smart Audio Guide:** GPS/bluetooth-enabled audio narration, offline mode, and downloadable content for on-site and remote experiences.
- **Community Stories:** Platform for visitors, monks, and locals to share experiences, reviews, and photos.
- **Custom Journey Planner:** Step-by-step tool to design personalized monastery tours, including guides, accommodations, and activities.

## Target Users

- **Tourists:** Seeking immersive, informative, and respectful experiences at Sikkim's monasteries.
- **Researchers:** Accessing digital archives, manuscripts, and AI-powered discovery tools.
- **Local Communities:** Sharing stories, promoting cultural events, and benefiting from sustainable tourism.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Vite
- **Mapping:** Leaflet.js for interactive maps
- **AI/ML:** Semantic search, auto-categorization, OCR for archives
- **Backend/Hosting:** Firebase (for authentication and data), static hosting

## Impact

- **Cultural Preservation:** Digitally safeguards centuries-old wisdom, art, and rituals.
- **Accessibility:** Opens up sacred spaces to those unable to visit in person.
- **Community Empowerment:** Supports local voices and sustainable economic opportunities.
- **Education:** Provides resources for learning about Buddhist heritage and Sikkimese culture.

## Vision

SoulOfSikkim bridges ancient wisdom with modern technology, ensuring that the spiritual treasures of Sikkim are preserved and celebrated for generations to come.
